Potential winners face Apache 2.0 licensing, response within one week, documentation within two weeks, and applicable tax, publicity/privacy and legal obligations.

Source: root's live complete Rules-page verification. This is an operational summary, not a substitute for the entrant reading and accepting those terms. The applicable age/majority and any exception or guardian-consent conditions must be checked for the entrant; this review does not infer a blanket under-18 rule from a shortened summary.

For this project, retaining original MIT notices is separate from agreeing to a conditional winner license. Do not replace existing notices or certify ownership automatically. ## Gemma experiments and proposal-only eligibility

**No mandatory Gemma-use or Gemma-inference experiment was found in the live paper-track overview or complete rules.** The overview expressly separates paper-track participation from the prediction competition. Its invitation to related research supports considering a measured systems/resource contribution. Restrictions on model variants and `agent.yaml` in the separate prediction competition should not be imported into the paper requirements.

That does **not** establish blanket acceptance of a proposal without completed experiments, nor does it guarantee relevance or a prize. No explicit proposal-only permission was verified. The required methods/experiments description and the judging criteria favor presenting what was actually implemented, measured and reproducibly checked.

The revised Suffice paper can therefore use its real runtime experiments as its contribution. It should explain a concrete developer-agent interface/use case, credit established techniques, and distinguish a proposed Gemma integration from measured runtime results. It must not call transport/session speedups Gemma inference speedups, agent task-success improvements or competition scores. This is editorial judgment from the verified scope and the available project evidence, not an eligibility ruling by the host.

## Actionable checklist for the revision

These manuscript checks remain pending until the final PDF/Writeup has been inspected; this research note does not falsely mark a future artifact compliant.

- [ ] Include every required section and explicit title/subtitle.
- [ ] Keep the complete text below 3,000 words with a documented conservative count; verify the final rendered/editor count.
- [ ] State one central research question and a specific contribution, rather than a release history or an untested agenda.
- [ ] Map central claims to actual retained experiments; define the comparator, useful-output contract, timing boundary, paired estimator and limitations.
- [ ] Explain why the resource matters to developer agents without inventing Gemma runs or agent outcomes.
- [ ] Support novelty through related work and precise differences; avoid portraying binary framing, LRU or immutability themselves as unprecedented.
- [ ] Preserve negative controls, uncertainty and threats to generalization alongside favorable results.
- [ ] Use citations and legible figures/tables; ensure the PDF and intended Writeup express the same claims.
- [ ] Describe supporting assets accurately: a retained-record audit is not a fresh native timing run, and AI-assisted review is not independent human peer review.
- [ ] If using a PDF or notebook project link, supply a genuinely public URL and check it without authentication. A local ChatGPT attachment is a deliverable, not proof of public Kaggle accessibility.
